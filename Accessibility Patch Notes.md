### Deaf and Hard of hearing accessibility
Deaf and Hard of hearing students may encounter trouble with these Test IDs:

#### Topic 16 Pre-Recorded Audio-Only, Video-Only, and Animations
- Test ID 16.A Audio-Only: requires transcripts are provided for pre-recorded audio-only content. Transcripts must be accurate and complete, including relevant sounds in addition to dialogue such as doors banging, sirens wailing, identification of speakers in dialogue, etc. Images of text are not acceptable as transcripts.
- Test ID 16.B Video-Only: requires that text or audio alternatives are provided for pre-recorded video-only content. The alternatives must include the same information that the video-only presentation displays (e.g., if the video includes multiple characters, the alternative must identify which character is associated with each depicted action). Images of text are not acceptable as text alternatives.

#### Topic 17 Synchronized Media
- Test ID 17.A Captions: requires that accurate captions are provided for pre-recorded synchronized media. Captions must be time-synchronized and equivalent, and include all non-dialogue audio information needed to understand the program content, including sound effects, music, laughter, speaker identification and location. The captions must also not obscure relevant video content.
- Test ID 17.B Audio Description: requires that, for pre-recorded synchronized media, the main soundtrack combined with audio descriptions adequately describe important visual details. This includes actions, characters, scene changes, onscreen text, etc. Transcripts and non-synchronized alternatives alone will not meet this requirement.
- Test ID 17.C Captions (live): requires accurate captions for live synchronized media. The captions must be time-synchronized and equivalent. Minor discrepancies in captions for live broadcasts may be acceptable due to limitations of real-time caption capabilities.
